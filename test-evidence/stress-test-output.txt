CrowdWatch - concurrency stress test (15 seconds)

Threads running at the same time:
  - simulated sensor feed updating counts every 1 ms
  - 2 reader threads: summary, areasByLoad, route, openIncidentsByPriority, unitsSorted
  - 2 writer threads: reportIncident, escalate, resolve, adjustOccupancy
  - 2 web clients: GET /, /api/areas, /api/incidents, /api/alerts, /api/route, POST /api/occupancy

Before (read methods not synchronized):
  errors: 51 (NullPointerException inside LinkedList/AVL tree while another thread was changing it)
  the simulated-feed thread crashed

After (every read method synchronized; GUI and reports hold the monitor's lock):
  operations: 21638, errors: 0, no thread hung (no deadlock)
